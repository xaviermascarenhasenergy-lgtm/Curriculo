I establish robust project controls to eliminate schedule slippage, manage commercial variations, and leverage digital tools like Excel and Power BI to optimize productivity—notably leading client-critical compliance and risk-mitigation programs to final sign-off. By combining high-level analytical capability with cross-functional leadership, I consistently deliver complex projects on target while ensuring strict contractual compliance and performance excellence.
